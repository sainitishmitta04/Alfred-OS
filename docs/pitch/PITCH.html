The Code · Trust & Safety

Alfred has a code.

  • Asks before anything irreversible: checked on the whole request, and again mid-task on the exact button
  • Answer by voice: only a whole “yes” or “no” counts, and questions expire after 10 minutes
  • Sandboxed: files only in Desktop, Documents and Downloads, and unsafe browser tools are blocked
  • Transparent: every step is live on screen and logged to SQLite with its latency
  • Never crashes: Jev → Claude → keywords, with model rotation, and a friendly answer every time
